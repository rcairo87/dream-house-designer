Dream House STEM Assignment

Design a functional home using the floor-plan tool. Every small square represents 1 foot.

  1. Create at least 3 bedrooms.
  2. Create at least 2 bathrooms.
  3. Add a kitchen and living room.
  4. Stay at or below the assigned square-foot limit.
  5. Add doors and windows so rooms can be entered and have light.
  6. Add furniture to show how each room will be used.
  7. Check the area of each room and total house area.
  8. Write a short explanation of your design choices.
